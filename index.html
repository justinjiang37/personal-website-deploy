2024 – Present · USC Makers

SkateMo

Autonomous Skateboard

A self-driving skateboard that navigates a route on its own. GPS supplies coarse global position and waypoints; LiDAR supplies the fine local geometry the GPS can't see. Fusing the two lets the board hold a commanded path to under 10 cm of accuracy while detecting and steering around obstacles in its way.

The interesting constraint is that both sensors are wrong in different ways — GPS drifts and updates slowly, LiDAR is precise but only knows what's directly in front of it. The fusion layer is what turns two unreliable signals into one trustworthy estimate.

LiDAR · GPS · Sensor Fusion · C++ · Embedded

2024 – Present · USC Makers

ATE Ball

Computer Vision Pool Table

A pool table that watches itself. An OpenCV and C++ pipeline detects the balls on the felt, predicts where the cue ball will travel after the strike, and projects that trajectory back down onto the table so the player can see the shot before taking it.

Runs the full detect → predict → project loop at 30 FPS — a ~33 ms budget per frame — while holding over 90% accuracy on predicted cue-ball paths.

OpenCV · C++ · Computer Vision · Projection Mapping
