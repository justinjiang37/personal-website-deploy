The Sampler.

A little beat maker. Finger-drum the pads (or your keyboard — 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V), toggle steps in the grid, then hit Play (or Space). Audio stays off until you flip Sound up top — nothing autoplays, and your pattern saves automatically.
